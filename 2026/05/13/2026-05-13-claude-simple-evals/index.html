Claude Evals: A Simple Evaluation Approach

Source Text

Why evals matter
Your work is unique. Claude might excel at drafting marketing copy but need more guidance for technical documentation in your specific domain. Running simple evals helps you:

  • Understand where Claude adds the most value in your workflow
  • Identify tasks where you’ll need to provide more context or examples
  • Build confidence in Claude’s outputs for recurring tasks

A simple eval approach
You don’t need complex infrastructure to evaluate Claude. Here’s a practical approach:

  • Gather examples.
  • Create test prompts.
  • Compare outputs.
  • Refine your approach.

Core Understanding

The key idea is:

Evals are lightweight evaluations used to test whether Claude performs well in your specific workflow, not just in general tasks.

evals 是一种轻量级评估方法，用来验证 Claude 在你的真实工作流中是否可靠，而不是只看它在通用场景下是否强大。

Claude may be strong at general writing, such as marketing copy, but may require more domain-specific context when working on technical documentation, QA analysis, storage-system debugging, Jenkins failure triage, or internal engineering communication.

Lightweight Scoring Rubric

You can score each Claude output from 1 to 5.

Final Summary

The full idea can be summarized as:

Use real work samples to test Claude, compare its output with your own high-quality examples, then refine prompts and context until you know which tasks are safe, valuable, and repeatable.

For technical work, especially QA/SDET and storage-system analysis, the most important rule is:

Do not trust fluent output by default. Build confidence through evals.
