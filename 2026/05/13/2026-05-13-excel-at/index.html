English Vocabulary: excel at

Source Sentence

Claude might excel at drafting marketing copy but need more guidance for technical documentation in your specific domain.

Core Meaning

Here, excel is a verb.

It means:

to be very good at something; to perform especially well in something

中文可以理解为：

擅长；表现出色；胜过一般水平

So the sentence means:

Claude 可能非常擅长撰写营销文案，但在你所在特定领域的技术文档写作上，可能需要更多明确的指导。

Important Distinction

Do not confuse the verb excel with Microsoft Excel.

Word Part of Speech Meaning
Excel noun / product name Microsoft Excel 表格软件
excel verb 擅长；表现优异
