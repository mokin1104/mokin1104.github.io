English Vocabulary Notes — Workplace & AI Language

2. Detailed Notes

wrestle with

Meaning: to struggle with a difficult problem, task, decision, or project.

Chinese: 艰难处理；努力解决；和问题“搏斗”。

Example:

I am wrestling with a large, complex project.

Workplace usage:

We are wrestling with several integration issues before the release.

amplify

Meaning: to make something stronger, larger, clearer, or more effective.

Chinese: 放大；增强；提升效果。

Example:

AI can act as a collaborator that amplifies your capabilities.

Nuance: amplify is stronger than help. It suggests that a tool or collaborator increases your output, speed, or problem-solving power.

expertise

Meaning: professional knowledge, skills, and experience in a specific field.

Chinese: 专业能力；专业知识；专长。

Example:

I have expertise in storage testing and root-cause analysis.

Related words:

Word Meaning
expert 专家
expertise 专业能力 / 专长
technical expertise 技术专长
domain expertise 领域专业知识

accomplish

Meaning: to successfully complete or achieve something, usually through effort.

Chinese: 完成；实现；达成。

Common collocations:

  • accomplish a task
  • accomplish a goal
  • accomplish a mission
  • accomplish an objective

Example:

What do you want to accomplish in this sprint?

one-off

Meaning: something that happens only once and is not repeated regularly.

Chinese: 一次性的；非周期性的；非长期的。

Examples:

This is a one-off task, not a recurring job.
This looks like a one-off failure.

indie

Meaning: short for independent.

Chinese: 独立的；独立制作的。

Examples:

  • indie film = 独立电影
  • indie music = 独立音乐
  • indie game = 独立游戏

Important note: indie does not mean India or Indian.

key competitors

Meaning: the most important competitors in a market.

Chinese: 主要竞争对手；核心竞争者。

Example:

The report should include market size, growth trends, and key competitors.

citations / cited

citation means a source reference.
cited means supported by citations.

Chinese:

  • citation = 引用；资料来源
  • cited report = 带引用来源的报告

Better expression:

a report with citations

deck

Meaning in business context: slide deck, usually a PowerPoint-style presentation.

Chinese: PPT；演示文稿；路演材料。

Examples:

  • investor deck = 投资人路演 PPT
  • sales deck = 销售演示文稿
  • pitch deck = 融资路演文稿

tailor

Meaning as a verb: customize or adjust something for a specific person, purpose, or need.

Chinese: 根据需求定制；按需求调整。

Common patterns:

tailor something to your needs
be tailored to your needs

Example:

We can tailor the solution to your needs.

shortcut

Meaning: a faster way to achieve something.

Chinese: 捷径；更快的方法。

Example:

Use context as a shortcut to understanding your needs.

Grammar note: Here, to is a preposition, so it is followed by understanding, not understand.

toggle on vs check

Phrase Meaning Chinese
toggle on switch from off to on 打开；启用
toggle off switch from on to off 关闭；禁用
check the logs inspect the logs 检查日志
check the box mark a checkbox 勾选复选框

Example:

Please toggle on this setting.
Please check whether this setting is enabled.

balance sophisticated capabilities with cost-effectiveness

Meaning: achieve a reasonable trade-off between advanced features and cost control.

Chinese: 在高级能力和成本效益之间取得平衡。

Example:

We need to balance sophisticated capabilities with cost-effectiveness when selecting an AI platform.

sophisticated

Meaning: advanced, complex, refined, or highly developed.

Chinese: 高级的；复杂精密的；先进的。

Examples:

  • sophisticated capabilities
  • sophisticated system
  • sophisticated analysis

cost-effectiveness

Meaning: whether something provides good value for its cost.

Chinese: 成本效益；性价比。

Important nuance: Cost-effective does not simply mean cheap. It means the result is worth the cost.

comprehensive

Meaning: complete and covering many important aspects.

Chinese: 全面的；综合的；覆盖充分的。

Examples:

  • comprehensive research
  • comprehensive report
  • comprehensive analysis

conduct

Meaning: to carry out or perform a formal activity.

Chinese: 进行；开展；执行。

Common collocations:

  • conduct research
  • conduct an investigation
  • conduct an experiment
  • conduct a survey
  • conduct an interview

Example:

ChatGPT can conduct comprehensive research and produce a cited report.

produce vs product

Word Part of Speech Meaning
produce verb 产出；生成
product noun 产品

Correct:

produce a report
conduct research
produce a research report

Incorrect or unnatural:

product research
produce research

well-crafted

Meaning: carefully designed, written, or organized.

Chinese: 精心制作的；精心组织的；认真打磨的。

Examples:

  • a well-crafted response
  • a well-crafted proposal
  • a well-crafted strategy

accuracy vs precision

Word Core Question Chinese Focus
accuracy Is it correct? 准确性 close to the true value
precision Is it consistent and exact? 精密度；精确性 stable, repeatable, fine-grained

Memory hook:

accuracy = 是否打中靶心
precision = 是否每次都打在同一个位置

Example:

The tool improves both accuracy and precision in log analysis.

precious

Meaning: very valuable and worth protecting.

Chinese: 珍贵的；宝贵的。

Examples:

  • precious time
  • precious resources
  • precious opportunity
  • precious memory

Nuance: precious often implies emotional value, rarity, or importance, not just high price.

previous

Meaning: earlier; before the current one.

Chinese: 之前的；上一个的；先前的。

Examples:

  • previous version
  • previous build
  • previous step
  • previous issue
  • previous result

accelerate / speed up

Expression Meaning Chinese Style
accelerate make something happen faster 加速；促进 formal / technical
speed up make faster 加快；加速 common / conversational

Examples:

AI can accelerate log analysis.
This script can speed up the testing process.

3. Workplace Example Sentences

  1. We are wrestling with a large, complex project, so we need a well-crafted plan.
  2. AI can act as a collaborator that amplifies our capabilities.
  3. We need to conduct comprehensive research and produce a cited report.
  4. The solution should balance sophisticated capabilities with cost-effectiveness.
  5. This automation can accelerate log analysis and improve test accuracy.
  6. The previous build failed, but we need more precision before identifying the root cause.
  7. This is a one-off task, not a recurring Jenkins job.
  8. Please toggle on the feature and check whether the setting is enabled.

4. Quick Review Checklist

  • accomplish = complete or achieve a goal
  • one-off = only once, not recurring
  • accuracy = correctness
  • precision = consistency and exactness
  • tailor = customize for a specific need
  • shortcut = faster way
  • toggle on = enable a switch
  • conduct research = do research
  • produce a report = create a report
  • well-crafted = carefully made or organized
  • comprehensive = covering many aspects
  • cited = supported by source references
  • deck = slide presentation
  • indie = independent
  • amplify = make stronger or more effective
