1. Core AI Vocabulary Table

English Term / Phrase Chinese Meaning Practical Meaning in AI Context Example
prompt 提示词；任务说明 The instruction or request you give to an AI model. A good prompt includes context, task, and rules.
effective prompt 有效 Prompt A prompt that helps AI produce useful, relevant, and well-structured output. Writing effective prompts improves response quality.
context 上下文；背景信息 Background information that helps AI understand your needs. Uploading logs gives the model context.
shortcut 捷径；更快理解的方法 Context acts as a faster path for AI to understand your needs. Documents are a shortcut to understanding the task.
background information 背景信息 Supporting information about your situation, project, or goal. Provide background information before asking for analysis.
relevant documents 相关文档 Documents directly related to the task. Upload relevant documents for better answers.
response 回答；响应 The model’s output after receiving your prompt. The response was too generic.
coworker 同事 A metaphor for how to communicate with AI naturally and clearly. Speak to Claude like you would a coworker.
naturally 自然地 Use normal human language instead of rigid commands. Ask naturally, but still be clear.
concisely 简洁地 Say enough, but avoid unnecessary words. Explain the task concisely.
conversationally 对话式地 Communicate through natural back-and-forth interaction. Use conversational prompts to refine the answer.
setting the stage 设定背景；搭建场景 Explain role, objective, audience, and context before the task. Setting the stage helps the model understand the situation.
role 角色 The identity or perspective used to guide the model. You are a senior storage SDET.
objective 目标 The final result you want to achieve. My objective is to identify the root cause.
defining the task 定义任务 Specify the exact action you want the model to perform. Define whether you want AI to write, analyze, build, or review.
task 任务 The concrete work you want AI to do. The task is to analyze the Jenkins log.
action 动作；操作 The specific verb-driven operation requested from AI. Analyze, summarize, rewrite, debug, design.
specifying rules 指定规则 Tell the model style, tone, constraints, and output requirements. Specify rules before asking for a report.
style 风格 The way the output is written or organized. Use a formal and concise style.
tone 语气 The emotional or professional attitude of the writing. Make the tone professional but apologetic.
example 示例 A sample that shows the model what output should look like. Provide an example format.
output format 输出格式 The required structure of the answer. Use Conclusion, Evidence, Risks, and Next Steps.
success criteria 成功标准 What counts as a good answer. The answer should be suitable for a Jira comment.
AI coworker AI 同事 A mental model: treat AI as a capable collaborator that needs clear instructions. Prompt = work order for an AI coworker.
collaborator 协作者 Something or someone that works with you. AI can be a collaborator.
amplify capabilities 放大能力；增强能力 AI helps you do more, faster, or better. AI amplifies your capabilities.
well-crafted response 精心组织的回答 A carefully structured and polished answer. Ask for a well-crafted response.
conduct research 开展研究 Perform systematic investigation. Claude can conduct comprehensive research.
comprehensive research 全面研究 Research covering multiple important angles. Conduct comprehensive research before writing the report.
cited report 带引用来源的报告 A report supported by source references. Produce a cited report.
citations 引用来源 References that support claims. Use current web research with citations.
current web research 当前网页研究 Research based on up-to-date web information. Use current web research for market analysis.
ground responses 让回答有事实依据 Base AI output on evidence, sources, or current information. Enable web search to ground responses.
web search 网页搜索 A tool for retrieving current information from the web. Enable web search for high-stakes facts.
multimodal 多模态 Ability to process more than one type of input, such as text and images. Claude can analyze text and visual elements.
text elements 文本元素 Written content in a document. PDF paragraphs are text elements.
visual elements 视觉元素 Images, charts, graphics, or visual layout. Claude can analyze visual elements in PDFs.
images 图片 Visual input such as PNG or JPEG files. Upload images for visual analysis.
charts 图表 Visual data representations. The model can analyze charts in a PDF.
graphics 图形；图像元素 Diagrams, illustrations, and non-text visuals. Graphics can provide context.
supported file types 支持的文件类型 File formats accepted by the model. PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, PNG, JPEG.
iterative 迭代式的 Improve step by step across multiple turns. Conversations with Claude are iterative.
chaining prompts 串联 Prompt Connecting multiple prompts to solve a larger task. Chain bite-sized prompts together.
bite-sized prompts 小块 Prompt Small, clear, manageable prompts. Use bite-sized prompts instead of one huge prompt.
natural dialogue 自然对话 A back-and-forth conversation that evolves over turns. Iterative prompts create natural dialogue.
guide the conversation 引导对话 Adjust direction based on model replies. Guide the conversation based on Claude’s replies.
replies 回复 The model’s previous answers in a conversation. Use replies to decide the next prompt.
generic response 泛泛的回答 An answer that is too general and not tailored to the situation. The response is too generic.
constraints 限制条件 Requirements or boundaries that shape the output. Add constraints to improve specificity.
length requirement 长度要求 Required answer length, such as word count or paragraph count. Keep this under 100 words.
format requirement 格式要求 Required answer structure. Use bullet points with bold headers.
confident-sounding 听起来很自信的 Output that sounds authoritative even if it may be wrong. Confident-sounding information can still be incorrect.
plausible 貌似合理的 Seems reasonable but may not be true. AI may generate plausible but incorrect facts.
incorrect information 错误信息 Information that is factually wrong. Verify incorrect or uncertain claims.
niche topics 小众/专门领域话题 Specialized domains where AI may be less reliable without context. Storage debugging can be a niche topic.
high-stakes work 高风险/高影响工作 Work where errors have serious consequences. Verify key facts for high-stakes work.
verify independently 独立验证 Check facts using another reliable source. Verify key facts independently.
confidence level 置信度 The model’s stated confidence in an answer. Ask Claude to indicate confidence level.
hallucination 幻觉；编造信息 Plausible but unsupported or false AI output. Hallucination risk increases with missing context.
fact 事实 A claim directly supported by evidence. Separate facts from assumptions.
assumption 假设 Something treated as possible but not proven. Label assumptions clearly.
inference 推断 A conclusion drawn from evidence. Rank inferences by evidence strength.
evidence chain 证据链 A sequence of observations supporting a conclusion. Build an evidence chain for root cause analysis.
AI Fluency AI 流利度；熟练使用 AI 的能力 Competence in using AI efficiently, effectively, ethically, and safely. The 4D Framework supports AI Fluency.
4D Framework 4D 框架 A framework with Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. The prompt framework is adapted from the 4D Framework.
Delegation 委派任务 Knowing what task to give to AI. Delegation means asking AI to do the right work.
Description 描述清楚 Explaining context, task, and constraints clearly. Description improves prompt quality.
Discernment 判断辨别 Evaluating whether AI output is accurate, useful, and safe. Discernment is required for high-stakes work.
Diligence 审慎负责 Using AI responsibly, ethically, and safely. Diligence prevents careless AI use.
effective AI use 有效使用 AI Using AI to achieve the intended outcome. Effective AI use requires clear prompts and verification.
ethical AI use 合乎伦理地使用 AI Using AI responsibly and fairly. Ethical AI use matters in professional contexts.
safe AI use 安全使用 AI Avoiding harmful, unsupported, or risky outputs. Safe AI use requires verification.
prompt debugging Prompt 调试 Improving prompts based on bad or unexpected outputs. Debug the prompt before blaming the model.
refine your approach 改进方法 Improve how you ask or guide the model. A bad response is a chance to refine your approach.
audience 受众 The people who will read the output. Specify the audience to control tone and depth.
authoritative 权威的；有分量的 Formal, confident, and credible in tone. Make this authoritative and formal.
formal 正式的 Professional and structured. Use a formal tone for client communication.
apologetic 带歉意的 Expressing apology or regret. Keep it professional but apologetic.

2. Core Concepts by Category

2.1 Prompt Construction

A good prompt usually includes:

Component Chinese Function
Role 角色 Sets the model’s perspective.
Goal 目标 Defines the final outcome.
Context 背景 Reduces guessing.
Task 任务 Defines the action.
Input 输入材料 Defines what evidence to use.
Rules 规则 Controls style, tone, constraints, and evidence discipline.
Output Format 输出格式 Makes the answer reusable.
Success Criteria 成功标准 Defines what a good answer should accomplish.

2.2 Context as a Shortcut

In AI usage, shortcut means:

a faster path to understanding your needs.

It does not mean a fragment or slice.

When you upload documents, logs, screenshots, or background information, the model can understand your situation faster and with less back-and-forth clarification.

2.3 Multimodal Understanding

Multimodal means the model can process multiple types of input.

In the current learning material, it mainly refers to:

Examples:

Modality Examples
Text PDF text, DOCX, TXT, CSV
Vision PNG, JPEG, charts, graphics, screenshots

Important nuance:

Multimodal input understanding does not necessarily mean the model can generate every type of output such as image, audio, or video.

2.4 Iterative Conversation

Iterative means step-by-step improvement.

AI conversation does not need one perfect giant prompt. A better pattern is:

Bite-sized prompts are small prompts that are easier for the model to handle.

2.5 Prompt Debugging

When the answer is not good, diagnose the prompt gap.

Bad Output Symptom Likely Prompt Gap Fix
Too generic Missing context Add role, audience, situation, and constraints.
Too long / short Missing length requirement Specify word count, paragraph count, or depth.
Wrong format Missing output structure Provide a format template.
Confident but wrong Missing factual constraints Ask for citations, evidence, and fact/assumption separation.
Wrong tone Missing tone guidance Specify tone or provide examples.

5. Quick Memory Rules

6. Final Checklist for Review

  • Can I explain what prompt means?
  • Can I explain why context is a shortcut?
  • Can I distinguish task, rules, and output format?
  • Can I explain iterative conversation and bite-sized prompts?
  • Can I explain what multimodal means?

7. One-Sentence Summary

AI communication improves when you provide clear prompts, enough context, explicit rules, useful examples, and iterative corrections based on the model’s replies.

中文：

AI 沟通质量取决于清晰的 Prompt、足够的上下文、明确的规则、合适的示例，以及基于回答持续迭代修正的能力。
